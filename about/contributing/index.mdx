# Contributing

If you'd like to contribute to Kord Extensions or any of its projects,
we have plenty of ways to get involved or support us:

- [Donate to support us.](https://donate.stripe.com/3csg29aPp95Rgxi7ss?client_reference_id=docs)
- <Link to="/about/contributing/code">Contribute code to our software projects.</Link>
- <Link to="/about/contributing/translations">Contribute translations.</Link>
- <Link to="/about/contributing/writing">Contribute writing work.</Link>
- <Link to="/bots/about/data-collection">Enable data collection in our Discord bot framework.</Link>
- <Link to="/about/community/rules-and-reporting#giving-feedback">Send us feedback.</Link>

Regardless, we appreciate it when folks use and enjoy our work, and we don't expect anything in return.
If you've used any of our tools and libraries in your projects, please let us know!
