# Our Community

To try to cater to our diverse community members, the Kord Extensions community exists in several places.
You can connect with us anywhere you prefer!

- **Bluesky:** [Our Bluesky profile](https://bsky.app/profile/kordex.dev/post/3lcfj62wlyk2y).
- **Discord:** [Our Discord server](https://discord.gg/nYzQWcjAmK).
- **GitHub:** Our [GitHub Organisation](https://github.com/Kord-Extensions),
  and [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Kord-Extensions/discussions).

We also plan to set up on the ActivityPub-based Fediverse at some point, but we're not ready for that yet.
